# Pack Check

List everything you bring to a sleepover or a trip, then tick each thing off when you pack to go home, so nothing gets left behind.

Use it here: https://irotheangel.github.io/pack-check/

- **Android app:** [PackCheck.apk](https://github.com/IroTheAngel/pack-check/releases/latest/download/PackCheck.apk). Open it on your phone; if Android asks, let your browser install unknown apps, then tap **Install**.
- Also in [Luv Launcher](https://irotheangel.github.io/luv-launcher/).

Your lists are saved on your own device. The whole app is `index.html`. Button icons are from Free Icon Pack v3.1 by @gvesster.
